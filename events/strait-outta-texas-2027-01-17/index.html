Lake Havasu Aquatic Center

**Strait Outta Texas – A Tribute to the King of Country, Just the Way George Did It**
Step into the timeless sound of pure country with **Strait Outta Texas**, the ultimate tribute to the one and only **George Strait**. From the very first note to the final encore, this band delivers an unforgettable experience that captures the heart, soul, and unmistakable sound of the King of Country—**just the way George did it**.
Every performance is a masterclass in authenticity, featuring the iconic fiddle runs, steel guitar bends, and smooth, signature vocals that defined Strait’s legendary career. Whether it’s a dancehall favorite like _“Amarillo By Morning,”_ a chart-topper like _“Check Yes or No,”_ or a timeless ballad like _“I Cross My Heart,”_ **Strait Outta Texas** performs each song with precision, passion, and genuine respect for the originals.
Perfect for country fans, dance halls, festivals, and true Strait die-hards, **Strait Outta Texas** isn’t just a tribute—it’s a heartfelt celebration of the man who defined a genre.
**No gimmicks. No shortcuts. Just the hits, the sound, and the spirit of George Strait.**
