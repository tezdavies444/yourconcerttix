The Moody Blues Tribute Concert

Orange Blossom Opry

Gordy Marshall, drummer for The Moody Blues for 25 years, has assembled some of the finest singers and musicians from the U.K. to create the ultimate celebration of one of classic rock’s most iconic bands.
Featuring an all-star line-up including Mick Wilson (10cc), Nick Kendall (Sting), and Patrick Duffin (West End production of _Jersey Boys_), this stunning production faithfully recreates the lush harmonies, soaring melodies, and orchestral magic that made The Moody Blues legendary.
Experience unforgettable classics including _Nights in White Satin_, _Tuesday Afternoon_, _Question_, _Forever Autumn_, _Isn’t Life Strange_, and _I’m Just a Singer (In a Rock and Roll Band)_ - alongside many more timeless hits.
If you love The Moody Blues, this is as close to the original sound as you will ever hear.
One unforgettable night. One extraordinary band. Don’t miss it.
