Satisfaction

The International Rolling Stones Show

Towerpoint RV Resort

Satisfaction is the international touring tribute show that celebrates the legacy of the World’s Greatest Rock & Roll Band – The Rolling Stones. Now entering its 25th year, this Billboard-acclaimed production has racked up over 4,000 performances across the globe, captivating audiences with its electrifying energy, authentic sound, and spot-on portrayal of Mick Jagger, Keith Richards, and the legendary Stones.
Performing up to 150 shows annually, Satisfaction has become a go-to favorite for top-tier venues including premier casinos, performing arts centers, music halls, and corporate clients across North America and beyond. With authentic costuming, era-accurate instruments, and a world-class cast, the production masterfully brings more than 60 years of Rolling Stones hits to life on stage. Satisfaction has earned widespread media recognition, with features in Rolling Stone
Magazine, Showbiz Magazine, Las Vegas Today, CBS Sunday Morning, and hundreds of other national outlets. It is consistently hailed as the definitive tribute to the Rolling Stones, honoring the band’s unmatched legacy with style and respect.
