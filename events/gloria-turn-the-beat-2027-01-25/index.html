Gloria! Turn The Beat

Gloria! Turn The Beat

Quail Creek

Get ready to experience the ultimate tribute to the Queen of Latin Pop with Gloria Estefan and the Miami Sound Machine – A Tribute, brought to you by TAD Management in collaboration with the incredibly talented Gabie Lopez! This electrifying show takes audiences on a high-energy journey through the unforgettable music of Gloria Estefan and the iconic Miami Sound Machine.

Gabie Lopez, an exceptional vocalist and performer, channels the essence of Gloria Estefan with her powerful vocals, dynamic stage presence, and infectious energy. From the first note to the last, Gabie and her talented band deliver an authentic and thrilling performance that brings Gloria’s greatest hits to life. You’ll be dancing in the aisles to unforgettable tracks like “Conga,” “Rhythm Is Gonna Get You,” “Anything For You,” and “1-2-3,” all recreated with stunning accuracy and passion.

With TAD Management’s expertise and Gabie Lopez’s incredible talent, this tribute show captures the magic and energy of the Miami Sound Machine, creating an unforgettable experience that will leave you cheering for more. Whether you’re a lifelong fan of Gloria Estefan or discovering her music for the first time, this show is a must-see celebration of her legacy and the timeless sound that made her an international sensation.
