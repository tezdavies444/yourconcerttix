Honky Tonk Man

The Dwight Yoakam Tribute

Monte Vista Village Resort

**Step into the honky-tonk heartbeat of country rock with _Honky Tonk Man_—the ultimate tribute to the pioneering sound of Dwight Yoakam.** From his signature Bakersfield twang to his chart-topping hits, this powerhouse show brings the style, swagger, and sound of a true country music legend to life.
Featuring spot-on vocals, blistering guitar licks, and a band that captures every bit of that hard-driving, toe-tapping energy, _Honky Tonk Man_ delivers hit after hit—from **“Fast as You”** and **“Ain’t That Lonely Yet”** to **“Streets of Bakersfield”** and, of course, the show-stopping **“Guitars, Cadillacs.”**
This tribute is more than just a concert—it’s a ride through the roots of modern country, celebrating the artistry and attitude that made Dwight Yoakam a genre-defying icon. Whether you're a die-hard fan or new to his music, you'll be singing along and two-stepping before the night is over.
**Put on your boots, crank up the twang, and let the legend live on.**
