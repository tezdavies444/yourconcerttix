Yachtzilla

The Monsters of Soft Rock

Sunland Village East

Get ready to set sail on a smooth, melodic journey with **Yachtzilla - The Monsters of Soft Rock**! This high-energy band delivers the best of yacht rock with a fun and infectious vibe that will have you singing along all night. Featuring a lineup of talented musicians who bring a fresh, modern twist to classic soft rock hits from the '70s and '80s, Yachtzilla is here to make every performance a memorable occasion.
From the smooth sounds of Christopher Cross to the feel-good tunes of Hall & Oates, Yachtzilla captures the essence of the era with impeccable style and charisma. Their lively shows are packed with harmonies, catchy melodies, and a groove that just won't quit. Whether you're a die-hard fan of the genre or discovering it for the first time, Yachtzilla promises a musical experience that's nothing short of spectacular.
So, don your captain's hat, grab a cocktail, and join Yachtzilla for a night of unparalleled fun on the high seas of soft rock!
