SH3

Pebble Creek Renaissance Theatre

Get ready for SH3, a stunning harmonic production filled with entertainment, glamour and powerhouse vocals. SH3 not only represents the entertainment capital of the world, but also brings together three Las Vegas headliners who have performed in some of the biggest shows on the Las Vegas Strip. The soulful songstress, Markeis Simon (Vegas the Show), the edgy rockstar, Anne Martinez (Bat out of Hell), the harmonic diva, Gabie Lopez (Cirque Du Soleil, creator and producer of SH3), bring the stylistic flavors of Vegas straight to your stage.
With shimmering costumes and unforgettable vocals, SH3 has something for every music enthusiast! Our three headliners bring the nostalgia of the past music eras to life, ensuring an unforgettable musical experience for all…from iconic pop anthems to soulful ballads and chart-topping rock classics, the best Latin hits, and some surprises! SH3 takes their versatile repertoire and put their “Vegas” twist on original arrangements that creates a one in a lifetime experiences for their audiences.
You no longer have to travel to see a Las Vegas Production, SH3 delivers all of the excitement, sparkle and energy right to you!
