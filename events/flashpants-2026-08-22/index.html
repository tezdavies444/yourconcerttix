FlashPants

Downtown Grand Hotel & Casino - NV

**FlashPants** is not just an 80s band—it’s a full-blown, high-energy experience that turns every event into a neon-soaked, sing-at-the-top-of-your-lungs celebration. From the first note to the final encore, FlashPants delivers the biggest hits of the decade with a Vegas-style production, larger-than-life personalities, and nonstop crowd interaction that keeps dance floors packed all night long.
Packed with iconic anthems, outrageous style, and infectious energy, the show transports audiences straight back to the era of big hair, bold fashion, and unforgettable music. Whether it’s _Don’t Stop Believin’_, _Livin’ on a Prayer_, or _Girls Just Want to Have Fun_, FlashPants doesn’t just play the hits—they bring them to life in a way that feels fresh, exciting, and impossible to resist.
Performing over 200 events a year, FlashPants has built a reputation for delivering consistently electric performances at corporate events, festivals, weddings, and private parties across the country. With hundreds of 5-star reviews and national television appearances, they’ve become one of the most in-demand 80s acts in America.
But what truly sets FlashPants apart is their ability to create a shared experience—one where every guest feels like part of the show. It’s more than music. It’s a party, a throwback, and a full-on celebration of one of the greatest decades in pop culture.
**FlashPants guarantees a night your audience won’t just remember—they’ll talk about it long after the lights come up.**
